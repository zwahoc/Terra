# Document family: surplus / excess / salvage / recovery
- Preserve whether a figure is a projected recovery, unused stock, candidate excess, declared potential surplus or source-declared confirmed surplus. These states are not equivalent.
- Record documented project/location, material details, raw quantities, release/availability dates and stated restrictions. Pre-demolition estimates do not prove that items were actually recovered.
- Only explicit source evidence supports `POTENTIAL_SURPLUS` or `CONFIRMED_SURPLUS`, and neither grants Terra approval, ownership entitlement, quality certification or reservable stock.
- No dedicated surplus document_type enum exists: use `OTHER` unless genuinely mixed.
