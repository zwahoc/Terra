# Document family: demand / requisition / outstanding material need
- Distinguish total planned requirement, new request, confirmed outstanding quantity and remaining local requirement. Map each line to a supported kind only when its wording establishes that distinction.
- Preserve requesting/destination project, material specifications, need-by date versus issue date and any actual fulfilment or commitment references.
- Do not calculate outstanding demand from planned quantity minus deliveries/commitments. Use `document_type=DEMAND` for genuine demand records.
